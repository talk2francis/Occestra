on-chain provenance — and you watch both happen, live, in ninety seconds.

**Recording setup:** 1920×1080, Chrome full-screen (hide bookmarks bar), cursor visible,
`prefers-reduced-motion` OFF, OpenAI credits topped up, demo credits available (check
/studio shows ≥ 2 remaining). Record with OBS or QuickTime; voice after, over the cut.

substitution test most often. Do a dry run first; if nothing fails on camera, the pack
pass rate line still tells the honesty story (fallback VO provided at beat 4).

## Capture checklist (record these exact screens)

1. `/studio` idle with presets visible — 3s hold before typing.
2. The fold-out animation on Run (do not cut it).
3. Feed lines: OpenStreetMap search + forecast (zoom 110% if text small).
4. First grade chip stamping in, then the pack pane settle.
5. A FAIL block with repair brief (or the fallback `/k` report).
6. Seal moment, uncut, then `/k` page + Verify going green.
7. `/#pricing` scroll + `/for-agents` curl block.
8. Hero with prism + end card (static, 3s).

**Rules:** no speed-ups on the money shot (4) or verify (5) — their realness is the pitch.
Everything else may be time-compressed with cuts, never with fake UI. No screen content
that isn't the live product.
