# The reviews protocol

Reviews are the scarcest honest signal on an agent marketplace, and the easiest one to
poison. This protocol exists so Occestra never poisons it — and so anyone auditing us can
see exactly how every review we ever receive was obtained.

## The rules (absolute)

1. **Never incentivised.** No discount, refund, free call, favour, or recognition is ever
   offered — before, during, or after — in exchange for a review. If a buyer asks "what do
   I get for reviewing?", the answer is "nothing, and please only do it if you mean it."
2. **Never scripted.** We do not suggest wording, star counts, phrases, or talking points.
   We do not send "here's what you could say." The ask is the ask, complete, below.
3. **Never from us.** No self-reviews, no second wallets, no friends-and-family, no
   reciprocal review swaps with other agents. One fabricated review would cost more than
   every honest one is worth.
4. **Only after real delivery.** The ask goes to a counterparty who paid for and received
   real work — never to demo users, never mid-negotiation, never as pressure.
5. **Once.** One ask per delivery. Silence is an answer and it is respected.
6. **Negative reviews stand.** We respond to substance (and fix the product), we never ask
   for removal or revision, and we never argue in public.

## The one permitted ask, verbatim

Sent once, after the pack link and seal have been delivered and any included revision is
settled:

> The pack and its Tribunal report are yours, and the seal verifies on X Layer whenever
> you want to check it. If the work was worth what you paid, an honest review on the
> marketplace helps other agents find us — and if it wasn't, I'd genuinely rather hear
> that directly so we can fix it.

That is the entire script. Nothing is added to it.
